ServiceNow Otto

Now Assist, Moveworks, and AI Experience, unified into one conversational front door that finishes the work — not just answers the question.

Just launched: Otto was announced at ServiceNow's Knowledge 2026 event and is rolling out across the platform through the rest of the year — live first inside ServiceNow EmployeeWorks and AI Control Tower. This page reflects Otto's public launch details; Exterprise's team is tracking the rollout closely as it reaches more products.

One front door, work actually finished

Most enterprises already have AI scattered across their tools — a copilot in the ticketing system, a chatbot on the intranet, a summarizer in the CRM — none of which talk to each other or remember the last conversation. ServiceNow Otto is ServiceNow's answer: a single conversational layer, built on its 2025 acquisition of Moveworks combined with Now Assist and the AI Experience interface framework, that understands intent and routes it to completion across departments and systems — instead of handing back an answer and leaving the rest to the employee.

Otto doesn't replace the workflows underneath — it sits on top of the same ServiceNow platform, approval chains, and permissions that were already there, and every action it takes is logged and made explainable through AI Control Tower. Exterprise's existing Now Assist for ITSM, HR Service Delivery, and Creator certifications, along with hands-on AI Agent Orchestrator and AI Control Tower implementation experience, cover the exact foundations Otto is unifying — putting us in a strong position to help clients prepare for it as the rollout reaches more of the platform.
