Modernized Field Service Management

The ServiceNow Field Service Management application helps organizations manage work tasks performed on assets in the field. It matches tasks to agents based on skills, location, technician availability, and parts/inventory. Agents can record details on assigned tasks like completion status, travel time to jobs, and add pictures and time spent.

Schedule Optimization — ServiceNow's AI-driven scheduling engine — takes this further, continuously re-matching the best-fit technician as conditions change: a cancellation, a delayed part, a higher-priority job. Its Explainability layer shows dispatchers exactly why each assignment was made, so the logic behind routes and timing is never a black box.

Two AI Agents extend this further: the Shift Management AI Agent closes coverage gaps automatically by matching available technicians to open shifts based on skills, location, and constraints, and the Create Work Order AI Agent turns a technician's field notes and photos into a fully structured work order in seconds — no manual data entry.

Exterprise's team of experts ensures that your Field Services team:

Solves customer issues on the first visit

Schedule Optimization automatically assigns the most qualified technician based on skills, location, availability, and parts on hand — with Territory Planning and Capacity & Reservations making sure the right crew is actually available before the job gets booked, not after.

Predictive Intelligence analyzes asset data, IoT sensor readings, and usage patterns to flag likely failures before they happen, automatically triggering preventive maintenance work orders instead of waiting on a breakdown.

Searchable offline, with Now Assist for Field Service surfacing similar past resolutions automatically.
